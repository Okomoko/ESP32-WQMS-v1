ESP32 Water Quality Helper PCB - Rev A
MECHANICAL DEFINITION

Board outline: 63.0 x 77.0 mm rectangular
Mounting holes: 4 x M3 plated/non-plated through holes, drill 3.2 mm
Hole centers: 3.5 mm from each board edge
Centers: (3.5,3.5), (59.5,3.5), (3.5,73.5), (59.5,73.5) mm
Remaining edge material outside drill: 1.9 mm nominal

ESP32 interface: two 15-pin, 2.54 mm pitch header rows; intended for common 30-pin ESP32 DevKit/WROOM-32 style boards.
Nominal row spacing: 25.4 mm.

Important: generic 30-pin ESP32 boards vary mechanically. The electrical pinout is fixed by the supplied 30-pin reference, but the exact DevKit PCB width/USB/antenna geometry should be checked against the user's physical board before fabrication.

Antenna: reserve a copper/component keepout around the ESP32 antenna end; no copper under/near antenna.

Rev A status: mechanical envelope locked; final manufacturing release still requires confirmation of the exact ESP32 DevKit mechanical variant and terminal block dimensions.
